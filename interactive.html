Interactive Apparel

WEAR IT.
TAP IT.

Selected Terencio Verde garments use embedded NFC technology to connect the physical piece to digital content.


The technology stays in the background. The clothing comes first.

◉

Tap the garment.

A phone can open a digital destination tied to that piece.

What it can unlock

More than a label.

Each garment can point somewhere different depending on the collection, artist, release or story behind it.

Artists

Open an artist profile, collaboration page or original work connected to the garment.

Music

Link a piece to a song, playlist, video or performance.

Stories

Give a design a deeper history, character profile or release story.

Special Drops

Send owners toward future releases, limited collections or private content.

Partners

Connect garments with collaborators, businesses and affiliate destinations.

Digital Worlds

Let selected pieces connect with Terencio Verde's Web3 and character-driven projects.

The idea

Technology without making the shirt feel like technology.

Terencio Verde uses NFC as an extra layer of the garment, not as the reason to buy it. The design should still stand on its own. The tap simply gives the piece somewhere else to go.
