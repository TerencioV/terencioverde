Interactive Apparel

WEAR IT.
TAP IT.

Selected Terencio Verde garments can carry embedded NFC technology that links a physical piece of clothing to a digital destination.

◉

Tap the garment.

A compatible phone can open content connected to that specific piece.

What it can unlock

More than a label.

The NFC layer can point somewhere different depending on the collection, artist, garment or story behind the piece.

Artists

Open an artist profile, collaboration page or original work tied to the garment.

Music

Connect a piece with a song, playlist, video or performance.

Stories

Give a design a history, character profile or deeper release story.

Special Drops

Send owners toward future releases, limited collections or special content.

Partners

Connect garments with collaborators, businesses and partner destinations.

Digital Worlds

Selected pieces can connect to Terencio Verde's character projects and Web3-inspired worlds.

Simple by design

Tap. Open. Explore.

The technology should feel easy enough that the customer doesn't need to think about the technology at all.

01

Wear

The NFC component is integrated into the garment while the design remains the main attraction.

02

Tap

Hold a compatible iPhone or Android device near the NFC area.

03

Explore

The phone opens the digital destination associated with that garment.

The principle

Technology without making the shirt feel like technology.

Terencio Verde uses NFC as an additional layer of the garment, not as the reason to buy it. The apparel has to work on its own. The tap simply gives the piece another dimension.
